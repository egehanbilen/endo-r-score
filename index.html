Research & educational prototype Implements a scoring system from a single-center pilot study (n = 46, biochemical-pregnancy endpoint). Not validated for clinical use; not tested against clinical pregnancy or live birth; must not guide treatment decisions. The 4.19 cut-off reflects apparent (in-sample) performance only.
